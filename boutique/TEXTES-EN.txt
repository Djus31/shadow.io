=== Title ===
SHADOW.io

=== Short ===
Hunt, raise a shadow army, slay Malakor.

=== Short description ===
Dark, fast .io: battle royale, raid, ×2 rush. Chests, armors, laser saber and pistol. Solo+bots, or real multi on a live server.

=== Long description ===
You drop as a hunter on a huge map. Open chests, swap weapons, raise shadows, outlast the zone.

Three modes
• Battle royale — last hunter standing.
• Raid — killing Malakor at the center ends the match. The slayer wins.
• Battle ×2 — the same hunt, double speed.

Weapons & style
Laser saber, katana, daggers, spear, maces, bow, bazooka, laser pistol. Armors, blades, shots and shadow trails unlock by playing. No signup: progress lives in your browser; a hunter code moves it to another device.

Controls
WASD move · Space attack · hold Space swap weapon · Shift dodge / sprint · R recall shadows · F merge · G duel.

Phones
On-screen stick and buttons.

Solo / multi
On this .io page: local match, bots fill empty slots.
Shared games (humans + bots) need the live server link.
